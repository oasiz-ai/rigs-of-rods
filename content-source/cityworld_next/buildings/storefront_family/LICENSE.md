# CityWorld Next storefront family licensing

The Blender sources, procedural generator, neutral GLB interchange files,
compiled OGRE resources, material scripts, object definitions, preview renders,
manifests, validators, and documentation in this family are project-authored by
Oasiz AI and Rigs of Rods contributors and distributed under
`GPL-3.0-or-later`.

No geometry, collision topology, material, texture, signage, trademark, or
shader from the user-supplied `CityWorld.zip` archive is included or imported.
The family contract records only compatibility facts obtained through a
read-only audit: object identifiers, placement counts, axis-aligned envelopes,
source yaw values, and cryptographic identities. These facts are used to
preserve scale and placement compatibility while the replacement art remains
independently authored.

The source archive remains external, byte-identical, and is not required to
generate or validate these assets.
